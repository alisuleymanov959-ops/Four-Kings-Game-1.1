⚔ FOUR KINGS ⚔




How to Play

• Play 2 players (a duel from opposite corners) or 4 players: the host chooses when creating the game. In 2-player games just destroy the enemy Town Hall.
• Destroy all enemy Town Halls. Last king standing wins.
• Rounds last 180 s. Everyone plans at once; orders resolve together when all players press End Turn or time runs out.
• You have 6 Action Points (8 after upgrading the Town Hall). Each move or attack costs 1 AP (Giant: 2).
• Click your unit, then click a blue cell to move or an enemy in range to attack. Click a Barracks to recruit; click buildings/units to upgrade. Cancel queued orders with ✕ (AP and gold are refunded).
• Gold: Town Hall +1, Gold Mine +7 (+9 upgraded) per round, gold deposits +3, kills +1, houses +4, Town Hall +25.
• Workshop (W) builds the Cannon (stationary, range 5, 2 AP per shot); upgrade it for 10g to build the Ram (buildings only, 35 dmg, move 2 AP / attack 3 AP) and Catapult (range 14, move 2 AP / attack 4 AP).
• Each kingdom is walled on its map-facing sides with two gates (G); only the owner's units can pass through its gates. Towers (▲) see 6 cells. Archers and crossbows cannot shoot through walls or trees (siege weapons can). Cannons are placed on walls. Walls upgrade for 1g (100 HP), gates for 2g (80 HP).
• Barracks Lv3 trains the Healer (heals an ally within 2 cells for 3 HP) and the Mage (range 3, 3×3 blast: 9 dmg centre, 8 around; also hurts your own units).
• Sounds are synthesized (no music): birds in sunny weather, wind, rain from round 4, combat and explosions. Mute in Settings or with the speaker button.
• Fog of war hides enemy units outside your vision.
• Four 2×2 Control Points (gold border) give +3 gold per round to whoever holds one uncontested. Ownership is only shown inside your vision (? = unknown, ⚔ = contested).
• Fireball unlocks in round 6 (3×3, 10 dmg), Fire Arrows in round 8 (4×4, 6 dmg).
• If you disconnect, reopen the page: your kingdom is kept for 5 minutes.
